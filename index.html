I am a second year graduate student at MIT, working with CSAIL’s Theory of Computation group. I am fortunate to be advised by Martin Rinard and Jonathan Kelner.

I am interested in dimensionality reduction and its application to randomized numerical linear algebra, sketching and streaming algorithms, and machine learning.

I also work on problems related to algorithmic graph theory and collaborate with MIT’s Center for Resilient Software on quantitative approaches to computer security and approximate computation.
